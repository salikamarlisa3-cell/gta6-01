AZURA HEAT — an original top-down 2D open-world crime game in vanilla JavaScript with zero network requests and all-original names, art, and sound. Roam Azura Bay's four districts on foot or in a stolen car (real driving physics, 15 AI traffic cars, 42 pedestrians who panic and flee), build a 0–5 wanted level from witnesses and face police chases, rams, BUSTED and WASTED outcomes, fight with a mouse-aimed pistol or melee, and work a five-mission story chain for fixer Marco Reyes as Vera Solano — plus taxi runs and checkpoint sprints on the side. Full HUD with minimap radar, title/pause screens, localStorage saves, a day-night cycle, and procedural WebAudio sound throughout. Built and playtested inside a sealed make.fun recording; the hand-in dossier is byte-identical across runs.

## Run it

node serve.ts
